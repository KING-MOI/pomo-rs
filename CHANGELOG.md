### Added
- `start` with configurable work, short break and long break lengths
- TOML config with `pomo config init|show|path`
- plain and bar display modes
- OSC 9, terminal-notifier and bell notifications
- session log with `pomo log` and `pomo stats` (streaks, per-day, per-task)
- keyboard controls and remote `pause|resume|toggle|skip|stop|status`
- daily goal, hooks, terminal title and shell completions
